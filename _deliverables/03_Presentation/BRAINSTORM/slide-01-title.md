# Slide 1 — Title (both, ~30s)

**Goal:** Say what Orbis is in one breath, say who built it, sound confident.

## Say
- "Good morning — we're Atakan and Arda. This is **Orbis**, an AI study-help system for students."
- "In one line: Orbis takes scattered university info and turns it into clear, traceable advice."
- "Our supervisor is Özgür Özdemir. This is for CMPE 492 at Istanbul Bilgi University."

## Show / point at
- The logo and the small ribbon: `University sources → Orbis → Student action`.

## Transition out
- "So why did we build it? Atakan will explain the problem." → hand to Atakan.

## If asked
- "Why the name Orbis?" → "It means orbit — the student is in the center, and the info comes to them."

## Don't
- Don't read the whole title block. Just say the one line.
- Don't go over 30 seconds.
