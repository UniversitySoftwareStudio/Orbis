# Slide 10 — Demo (both, ~60s)

**Goal:** Show the three paths in one short story: Ask → Obligation → Submit. End on the tagline.

## Say
- "Let's see it all work, in three steps."
- "**Ask** — a student asks 'How do I apply for Erasmus?' and gets a streamed answer **with source links**."
- "**Obligation** — on My Regulations, the student already has personal to-dos pushed to them, each with its source and how urgent it is."
- "**Submit** — they upload an assignment. The checker goes through each requirement live, shows the proof, and gives a verdict — with an appeal option if it's wrong."
- "Closing line: **study help that comes to you, that you can check, and that we can measure.** Thank you — questions?"
