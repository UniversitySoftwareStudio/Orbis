# Slide 2 — Problem Statement (Atakan, ~55s)

**Goal:** Make people feel the gap — the info exists, but it just sits there and never reaches the right student at the right time.

## Say
- "University info already exists. The problem is it's **spread out and passive**. It lives in the SIS, course catalogs, regulation PDFs, and announcements."
- "The real problem isn't search. Students don't know **what to ask** or **when to act**."
- "A deadline in a PDF doesn't help you if nothing tells you it's *your* deadline, *today*."
- "Today's portals just **store records**. They don't look at your situation."
- "So we need help that comes to you, fits your case, and shows its source. That's the gap Orbis fills."

## Show / point at
- The map of scattered sources, then the student in the middle with the two gaps: "what to ask" and "when to act".

## Transition out
- "Here's how we close that gap — Arda." → hand to Arda.

## If asked
- "Don't advisors already do this?" → "Yes, but they can't track every rule for every student all the time. Orbis helps the advisor, it doesn't replace them."
- "Doesn't the school chatbot (HermiOne) do this?" → "That one only answers when you ask. Orbis tells you *before* you ask."

## Don't
- Don't show numbers here. This slide is about the problem. Numbers come on slides 6–7.
- Don't say old systems are "useless." Say they're passive and just store records.
